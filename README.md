**Palm Shores** is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser — a
full **3D WebGL sunset city** built on a vendored three.js, with a chase camera behind the
wheel and a third-person camera on foot. One coastal city of art-deco blocks, palm-lined
avenues, a beach, a bay and the Pier Nine docks: traffic keeps its lanes and turns at the
lights, pedestrians stroll the block sidewalks and scatter when trouble starts, and the
**Shores PD** answers a 1–5 star wanted level with cruisers that pursue, ram and set up
physical roadblocks, plus the **AIR-1 OSPREY** helicopter holding its searchlight on you
from three stars up. Everything is procedural — the city, the facade textures, the
billboards, the radio music and the engine note are generated by the game's own code at
boot. No build step, no network requests, no image or audio files: every asset is code.

The HUD reads like the reference the crew works from. Top-left, the **mission dossier** —
the current chapter (`CH 7 — PAPER MOON`), its objective, a condition line, a segmented
stage bar, what comes next and the district — over a **Shores PD response panel** that
names every responding unit (IBIS-43, TARPON-19, HERON-17…), each with its live status —
RAMMING, PURSUIT, INTERCEPT, ROADBLOCK, AIR SUPPORT — its real distance, a count of the
units the panel cannot show (`+2 more units on Tac-3`), and an IN SIGHT / EVADING flag.
**Luna** speaks from a dispatch dialogue box with her own portrait. Bottom-left, a **round
minimap** draws the road grid, an amber route to the objective, police blips and the
Sunset Stars, over RESPECT, XP and HEALTH bars. Bottom-right, the radio (four stations)
and the cockpit: vehicle name, condition and nitro bars, a big speedometer and gear
(D1–D5). Top-right: the DAY 1 clock, cash, the five wanted stars and the three weapon
slots — earned guns lit, the rest stamped LOCKED (KESTREL 9, WASP 6149, MARLIN 12).

## The story — 8 jobs for Luna

Play as **Mara Voss**, the fastest wheel in Palm Shores. Eight jobs stand between her and
owning the Bayline Garage:

1. **Salt & Static** — collect your Vespera GT on Coral Strand and run a fragile package to the Hotel Solana.
2. **Borrowed Time** — lift a debtor's coupe in Palm Heights and bring it home against the clock while the PD warms up.
3. **Tail Lights** — tail the Velmara crew's courier across town without losing him. *(pays out the WASP 6149)*
4. **Pier Nine** — clear three armed rivals off the docks on foot and take back the ledger.
5. **Heat Line** — lift a laden taxi downtown, lose the Shores PD, park it at Bayline. *(pays out the MARLIN 12)*
6. **The Long Goodnight** — crash a villa party on the Strand, then stop the fleeing driver.
7. **Paper Moon** — take the Pier Nine payroll, lose a locked 4-star response — helicopter, roadblocks and all — before Luna's scanner window closes, then bring the money home.
8. **Star Run** — six checkpoints against a rival coupe. Winner takes the garage.

Between jobs the city is yours: **carjack anything** parked or crawling, hunt the twelve
**Sunset Stars** (+25 XP each), and spend your earnings at **Bayline Garage** — a street
tune, a nitro kit and reinforced panels for the Vespera, fitted for good. Cash, XP,
RESPECT, stars, upgrades and story progress all persist in a localStorage save.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Drive / walk |
| Mouse (click the game for pointer lock) | Aim on foot |
| Left click | Shoot |
| F | Enter a car / get out / carjack |
| E | Bayline Garage tuning (in the Vespera, at the bay) |
| Q (hold) | Weapon wheel — pick with the mouse, release to confirm; tap to cycle |
| R | Reload |
| Space | Handbrake |
| Shift | Sprint on foot / nitro in the Vespera (once fitted) |
| N | Next radio station |
| M | Mute |

## Run it

Play locally with the bundled static server (Node 22, standard library only):

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8123
# open http://localhost:8123
```

Build the hand-in dossier (copies `game/` and `screenshots/` and writes `dossier.json`):

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Deterministic screenshots of the game itself live in `screenshots/` and are described by
`shots.json` — each is a real render staged by the game's own `?shot=<scene>&seed=7` mode
(the cover is the Paper Moon chase, staged exactly as it plays out in the story).
